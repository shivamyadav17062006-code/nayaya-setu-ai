# Official State Legislation Sources & Statutory Register

## 1. Statutory Source Hierarchy

To ensure 100% legal traceability and zero hallucination, NyayaSaathi references only official government sources for Indian State and Union Territory legislation:

1. **State Official e-Gazettes**: Primary publication of enactment, amendments, rules, and commencement notifications.
2. **State Legislative Assembly Repositories**: Authoritative text of passed Bills and Acts.
3. **India Code State Legislation Records**: Official Ministry of Law and Justice consolidated digital records (`https://www.indiacode.nic.in`).
4. **State Legal Services Authorities (SLSAs)**: Statutory income threshold notifications and regulations issued under Section 12(h) of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987.

## 3. Universal Statutory Non-Income Exemptions

Under **Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987**, the following citizens are entitled to free legal aid across **every State and Union Territory** without regard to any income threshold:
* Every woman and child.
* Members of Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST).
* Persons with disabilities (under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016).
* Victims of human trafficking or *begar* (forced labour).
* Victims of mass disaster, ethnic violence, caste atrocity, flood, drought, earthquake, or industrial disaster.
* Industrial workmen.
* Persons in custody or in a protective custody home.
