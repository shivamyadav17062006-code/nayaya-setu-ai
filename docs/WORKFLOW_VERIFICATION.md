# NyayaSaathi (न्यायसाथी) — Statutory Workflow Verification Register

## 1. Statutory Verification Standards

In accordance with `AGENTS.md` Rule 6 (*Zero Legal Hallucinations & Grounded Citations*), every procedural workflow in NyayaSaathi must satisfy:
1. **Direct Statutory Grounding**: Rooted in enacted Indian Acts, Central Rules, and High Court / Supreme Court precedents.
2. **Official Government Domains Only**: External URLs restricted strictly to official government portals (`.gov.in` and `.nic.in`).
3. **Verified Timelines**: Limitation periods and statutory deadlines reflect the actual statutory provisions, not estimates.
4. **Transparent Fee Schedules**: Complete breakdown of statutory court fees, stamp duties, and mandatory legal aid waivers under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987.

## 2. Verified Procedural Workflows Catalog

### Workflow 1: Recovery of Unpaid Salary or Delayed Wages
- **Category**: `LABOUR`
- **Statutes**:
  - Payment of Wages Act, 1936 (Sections 5 & 15)
  - Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (Section 33C)
  - Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Free Legal Aid)
- **Responsible Authorities**: Office of the Labour Commissioner, Conciliation Officer, Labour Court, DLSA
- **Official Portal**: `https://shramsuvidha.gov.in`
- **Verified Timelines**: Wages due on 7th/10th of following month; 12-month limitation under Section 15(2); 30-day conciliation period.
- **Statutory Fees**: Free for claims under ₹1,600/month; nominal ₹10–₹50 stamp duty or completely free via DLSA.
- **Appellate Authority**: District Court under Section 17 of Payment of Wages Act (within 30 days).

---

### Workflow 2: Protection Order, Residence & Shelter against Domestic Abuse
- **Category**: `DOMESTIC_VIOLENCE`
- **Statutes**:
  - Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 (PWDVA — Sections 12, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22)
  - Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Sec. 12(c) unconditional entitlement)
- **Responsible Authorities**: Emergency 112, Sakhi One Stop Centre, Taluka/District Protection Officer, Judicial Magistrate First Class (JMFC)
- **Official Portal**: `https://nalsa.gov.in` / `https://wcd.nic.in`
- **Verified Timelines**: Emergency response immediate; First Magistrate hearing within 3 days; Application disposal within 60 days.
- **Statutory Fees**: Free of cost under Section 12 of PWDVA.
- **Appellate Authority**: Sessions Court under Section 29 of PWDVA (within 30 days).

---

### Workflow 3: Consumer Redressal for Defective Products or Deficient Services
- **Category**: `CONSUMER`
- **Statutes**:
  - Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Sections 34, 35, 38 & 47)
  - Consumer Protection (Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions) Rules, 2020
  - Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
- **Responsible Authorities**: National Consumer Helpline (NCH), District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission
- **Official Portal**: `https://edaakhil.nic.in` / `https://consumerhelpline.gov.in`
- **Verified Timelines**: 2-year limitation period from date of cause of action; 21 days for admissibility; 90 days for disposal without testing.
- **Statutory Fees**: Nil fee up to ₹5 Lakhs claim value; ₹200 for ₹5L–₹10L; ₹400 for ₹10L–₹20L; ₹1,000 for ₹20L–₹50L.
- **Appellate Authority**: State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission within 45 days with 50% deposit.

---

### Workflow 4: Reporting Online Financial Fraud, UPI Theft & Cybercrime
- **Category**: `CYBERCRIME`
- **Statutes**:
  - Information Technology Act, 2000 (Sections 43, 66C, 66D, 70B)
  - Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Cheating and identity fraud sections)
- **Responsible Authorities**: National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (NCRP), Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System (CFCFRS - 1930), Cyber Crime Police Station
- **Official Portal**: `https://cybercrime.gov.in`
- **Verified Timelines**: Golden Hour reporting within 2 hours of fraud for bank freeze; Acknowledgement within 24 hours.
- **Statutory Fees**: Free of cost.
- **Appellate Authority**: State Cyber Appellate / High Court Criminal Revision.

---

### Workflow 5: Workplace Sexual Harassment Complaint (POSH)
- **Category**: `WORKPLACE_HARASSMENT`
- **Statutes**:
  - Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH Act)
  - Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Rules, 2013
- **Responsible Authorities**: Internal Committee (IC) / Local Committee (LC), Ministry of Women & Child Development (SHe-Box)
- **Official Portal**: `https://shebox.wcd.gov.in`
- **Verified Timelines**: Complaint within 3 months of incident (extendable by 3 months); Inquiry completed within 90 days; Employer implementation within 60 days.
- **Statutory Fees**: Free of cost.
- **Appellate Authority**: Appellate authority under service rules or Industrial Tribunal within 90 days under Section 18.

---

### Workflow 6: Filing Right to Information (RTI) Application & Appeals
- **Category**: `RTI`
- **Statutes**:
  - Right to Information Act, 2005 (Sections 6, 7, 18 & 19)
  - Right to Information Rules, 2012
- **Responsible Authorities**: Central/State Public Information Officer (CPIO/SPIO), First Appellate Authority (FAA), Central/State Information Commission (CIC/SIC)
- **Official Portal**: `https://rtionline.gov.in` / `https://cic.gov.in`
- **Verified Timelines**: Normal response: 30 days; Life or Liberty: 48 hours; First Appeal: 30 days; First Appeal decision: 30 to 45 days; Second Appeal: 90 days.
- **Statutory Fees**: ₹10 application fee for Central Government; Nil fee for citizens Below Poverty Line (BPL).
- **Appellate Authority**: High Court under Article 226 (Writ Jurisdiction against final Commission order).

---

### Workflow 7: Senior Citizen Maintenance Claim & Protection from Eviction
- **Category**: `SENIOR_CITIZENS`
- **Statutes**:
  - Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007 (Sections 4, 5, 9 & 23)
- **Responsible Authorities**: Maintenance Tribunal presided over by Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM), Conciliation Officer, Elderline (14567)
- **Official Portal**: `https://socialjustice.gov.in`
- **Verified Timelines**: Notice issued within 15 days; Final order within 90 days from notice service.
- **Statutory Fees**: Free of cost; advocates prohibited without tribunal permission under Section 17.
- **Appellate Authority**: Appellate Tribunal presided over by District Magistrate (DM) within 60 days under Section 16.
