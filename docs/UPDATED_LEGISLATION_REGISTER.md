## 3. Statutory Grounding for Public Citizen Rights

### A. Wages & Employment Rights
- **Operative Law Today**: Payment of Wages Act, 1936 (Sections 5 & 15); Minimum Wages Act, 1948 (Sections 3, 12, 20); Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (Section 33C).
- **Enacted Modern Code**: Code on Wages, 2019 (Sections 16, 18, 45).
- **Procedural Venue**: Assistant Labour Commissioner / Designated Payment of Wages Authority / Labour Court.

### B. Criminal Safeguards & Arrest Rights
- **Offenses on/after 01-07-2024**: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Sec. 85, 316) & Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (Sec. 35, 47, 58).
- **Offenses before 01-07-2024**: Indian Penal Code, 1860 (Sec. 498A, 406) & Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (Sec. 41, 50, 57).
- **Constitutional Anchor**: Article 22(1) (Right to consult legal practitioner) & Article 22(2) (24-hour Magistrate production).

### C. Legal Aid Entitlements
- **Governing Law**: Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Act No. 39 of 1987).
- **Section 12 Non-Income Exemptions**: Women, children, SC/ST, custody inmates, persons with disability, victims of trafficking, and industrial workmen qualify for 100% free legal aid regardless of income.
- **National 24x7 Helpline**: **15100** (NALSA).
