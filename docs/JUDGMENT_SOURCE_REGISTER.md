# Official Judgment Source Register & Precedent Catalog

## 1. Statutory Source Policy

In accordance with **AGENTS.md** rules, NyayaSaathi references exclusively official judicial publication sources:
* **Supreme Court of India Official Repository**: `https://main.sci.gov.in` and the official Electronic Supreme Court Reports (e-SCR) `https://escr.sci.gov.in`.
* **State High Court Official Portals**: Certified official PDF judgments uploaded by the Registrars of High Courts (e.g., Bombay High Court `bombayhighcourt.nic.in`, Delhi High Court `delhihighcourt.nic.in`).
* **Document Checksums**: Every ingested case law document is fingerprinted with a 64-character SHA-256 checksum to ensure tamper evidence.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Honest Scope Notice**: NyayaSaathi does not claim comprehensive coverage of all hundreds of thousands of Indian judgments. This pilot knowledge base covers **landmark constitutional and statutory precedents** that directly define citizen rights in labour, gender equality, domestic violence, custodial liberty, and consumer protection.

## 3. Statutory Provision Cross-Reference Index

* **Constitution of India**:
  * **Article 14** (Equality & Non-Arbitrariness): *Vishaka* (1997), *State of Punjab v. Jagjit Singh* (2016).
  * **Article 19(1)(g)** (Right to practice profession free of harassment): *Vishaka* (1997).
  * **Article 21** (Life, Personal Liberty & Fair Procedure): *Vishaka* (1997), *Hussainara Khatoon* (1979), *D.K. Basu* (1996).
  * **Article 39A** (Equal Justice & Free Legal Aid): *Hussainara Khatoon* (1979).
* **Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 (PWDVA)**:
  * **Section 2(s)** (Shared Household) & **Section 17** (Right to Reside): *Satish Chander Ahuja v. Sneha Ahuja* (2020) [Overruling *S.R. Batra*].
* **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (POSH) Act, 2013**:
  * **Section 4** (Internal Complaints Committee Mandate): *Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan* (1997) [Precursor and origin of legislation].
* **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**:
  * **Section 2(42)** (Definition of "Service"): *IMA v. V.P. Shantha* (1995) [Medical negligence covered under consumer forums].
* **State Labour Welfare Legislation**:
  * *Maharashtra Shops and Establishments Act, 2017* (Sections 6 & 13 mandatory overtime pay): *State of Maharashtra v. Bharatiya Hotel* (2018).
