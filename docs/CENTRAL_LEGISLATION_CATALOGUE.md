## 2. Ingestion & Preservation Quality Standards

1. **Original Source Preservation:** Every statutory document preserves its official gazette number, India Code identifier, and verified URL.
2. **Deterministic Checksumming:** All documents in the central corpus are validated with cryptographic SHA-256 signatures (`data/legislation/central_source_register.json`).
3. **Traceable Hierarchy:** Provisions are indexed as enforceable sections, subsections, and clauses without fabricating section numbers or modifying statutory wording.
4. **Subordinate Linkage:** Subordinate Rules and Regulations explicitly reference their enabling parent Act and provision.
