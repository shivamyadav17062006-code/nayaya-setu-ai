# NyayaSaathi (न्यायसाथी) — Legal Aid Official Sources & Statutory Limits

## 1. Statutory Framework

Legal Aid in India is governed by:
- **Constitution of India**:
  - Article 39A: Equal justice and free legal aid.
  - Article 21: Fundamental Right to life and personal liberty (including right to free legal representation for undertrials, established in *Hussainara Khatoon v. Home Secretary, State of Bihar (1979)*).
  - Article 22(1): Right to consult and be defended by a legal practitioner of choice.
- **Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Act No. 39 of 1987)**:
  - Section 12: Criteria for giving legal services.
  - Section 13: Entitlement to legal services.
  - Section 28: Power of State Governments to make rules in consultation with the Chief Justice of the High Court.
- **National Legal Services Authority (Free and Competent Legal Services) Regulations, 2010**.
